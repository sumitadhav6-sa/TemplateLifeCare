Care organised around what you actually need

From a routine check-up to ongoing management of a chronic condition, our departments work together instead of passing you between waiting rooms.

General & Family Medicine

Routine check-ups, vaccinations, and long-term care for every member of the family, with doctors who follow your history year over year.

Diagnostics & Lab Testing

On-site blood work, imaging, and screening panels, with most results available within the same day so treatment isn't delayed.

Specialist Consultations

Cardiology, dermatology, orthopaedics, and paediatrics available in-clinic, so referrals don't mean starting over somewhere new.

Urgent & Walk-in Care

Same-day slots held aside every day for injuries, infections, and anything that can't wait for a scheduled appointment.
